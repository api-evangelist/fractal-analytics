# Fractal Analytics

> Fractal Analytics Limited (fractal.ai) is an enterprise AI services and products company founded in 2000, headquartered in New York with a large delivery footprint in India and roughly 5,000 employees across 18 global locations. It sells AI consulting, data engineering, decision-science and design services to Fortune 500 clients, and builds products on top of that practice — most notably Cogentiq, its enterprise agentic AI platform. Fractal listed on the NSE and BSE under the symbol FRACTAL in February 2026.

## APIs

Fractal Analytics publishes **no public API**. As of 2026-08-04 there is no developer portal,
no API reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no published SDK or CLI, no hosted MCP
server and no A2A agent card reachable anonymously on any Fractal-controlled host. API access is
delivered inside client engagements and gated product tenants (the Cogentiq tenant host
cogentiq.fractal.ai answers 502 from its gateway to anonymous requests).
